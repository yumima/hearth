AI models on your own computer

hearth finds a model that suits your machine, checks that it works there, and serves it to your programs on one local address. mantel is its chat window. Nothing you type leaves the computer.

Install

Linux (x86-64) and macOS (Apple Silicon):

curl -fsSL https://yumima.github.io/hearth/install.sh | sh

Windows, in PowerShell:

irm https://yumima.github.io/hearth/install.ps1 | iex

For you alone, without administrator rights, in one folder. It leaves an Ollama, a Python or a hearth you already have as they are. Read the script first if you like. The engine is up to 2 GB, and the model you choose 3 to 18 GB.

After that

The setup page opens. It lists the models that suit your computer, best first. The one you choose is downloaded, compared with the build that was tested, loaded with as much room as your graphics card has for it, measured and checked before any program gets it.

Programs reach it at http://127.0.0.1:11435/v1, where they expect OpenAI. finterm finds it by itself and asks.

mantel

The chat window that comes with it: your own tools through MCP, voice in and out, a language tutor. It also works with other engines. Leave it out with --no-mantel.

What it runs on

ComputerWhat to expect
Graphics card with 11 GB or more, or a Mac with 24 GBA capable model, entirely on the GPU
Graphics card with 3 to 11 GB, or a Mac with 8 GB or moreA smaller model, entirely on the GPU
16 GB of memory and no such cardA small model, slowly

Run by us on NVIDIA under Linux. AMD cards, Windows with a graphics card, and Macs are experimental until somebody has told us how they do.

Updating and removing

hearth update brings the newest release; one that does not start is taken back by itself. hearth uninstall removes it, and can keep the models.
